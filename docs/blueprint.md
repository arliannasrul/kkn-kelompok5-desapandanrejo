# **App Name**: Pandanrejo KKN Archive

## Core Features:

- Landing Page & Overview: A welcoming landing page featuring an introduction to KKN Kelompok 5 Unmer Malang, Desa Pandanrejo, and a brief overview of their mission.
- Organization Structure Display: A dedicated section to visually represent the KKN group's organizational structure and team members.
- Work Programs Showcase: List and describe the various work programs carried out by the KKN group, highlighting key activities and goals.
- Archived Outputs Gallery: Categorized display for KKN outputs including blog articles, PKM AI documents, posters, and video profiles.
- Integrated Document & Media Preview: Allow users to preview existing files directly on the website for articles, PKM AI documents, posters (images), and embedded video profiles.
- AI Content Summarizer Tool: An AI-powered tool to generate brief summaries or key takeaways for uploaded articles and PKM AI documents, aiding in quick content understanding.
- Interactive UI Animations: Implement subtle and purposeful interactive animations to enhance user engagement and provide a modern feel to the website's navigation and content display.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Color: An earthy forest green (#668F3D), chosen to reflect the natural landscape of Desa Pandanrejo and evoke a sense of growth and community.
- Background Color: A very light, desaturated green (#F2F5F0) that provides a clean and calming backdrop, ensuring high readability and a fresh aesthetic.
- Accent Color: A vibrant blue-cyan (#52E0E0), used to highlight interactive elements, calls-to-action, and add a touch of modern dynamism that contrasts beautifully with the primary green.
- Headline Font: 'Playfair' (serif) for an elegant, contemporary, and high-end feel in titles and headers. Body Font: 'PT Sans' (humanist sans-serif) for excellent readability and a warm, modern touch in longer text passages like articles and descriptions.
- Use a set of modern, clean line icons. Prioritize icons that subtly relate to themes of community, documentation, project milestones, and archival content to maintain a consistent visual language.
- Employ a clear, structured, and responsive grid-based layout. Emphasis on easy navigation and hierarchical content presentation for all documentation and archival elements, ensuring optimal viewing across devices.
- Incorporate subtle, well-timed animations such as soft fade-ins for content sections upon scroll, gentle hover effects on interactive elements, and smooth transitions when navigating between different sections or categories of archives.
